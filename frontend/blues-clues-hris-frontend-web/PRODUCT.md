# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

HR managers, interviewers, and recruiters at Philippine companies. They manage employee records, post jobs, screen applicants, handle timekeeping, and run onboarding. Mid-level professionals who value clarity and speed over technical sophistication. Decision-makers (owners, HR heads) also evaluate the subscription — they want ROI confidence and a trustworthy vendor, not flashy feature lists.

## Product Purpose

Blue's Clues HRIS is a complete HR management platform built for Philippine companies. It consolidates hiring, onboarding, timekeeping, performance, and workforce analytics into one platform — replacing spreadsheets and patchwork tools. Success looks like an HR team that can run their full people-ops workflow without leaving the product.

## Brand Personality

Friendly, approachable, and simple. Competent without being cold. Professional enough to earn trust from executives, warm enough that HR staff actually enjoy using it. Voice is direct and practical — like a capable colleague, not a corporate manual.

## Anti-references

- **Generic SaaS cream**: white backgrounds, gray body text, Inter everywhere, no visual identity — looks like every bootstrapped B2B tool. Avoid the beige-neutral palette trap.
- **Dark mode tech dashboards**: neon-on-dark, cyberpunk/ops-tool aesthetic — wrong register for HR buyers in the Philippines.
- **Over-illustrated HR tools** (BambooHR, GreatDay): cheerful cartoons and pastel playfulness undercut credibility.

## Design Principles

1. **Clarity over cleverness** — every screen should answer "what do I do next?" without hunting.
2. **Confident but not cold** — professionalism that still feels human; the product should feel like it's on your side.
3. **Local-first** — small signals (peso signs, Philippine company context, familiar payment methods) build trust with the actual audience.
4. **Progressive simplicity** — start simple, reveal complexity only when needed. Don't overwhelm HR managers who aren't power users.
5. **Trust is earned at every step** — especially in the subscription flow: security signals, clear pricing, and zero ambiguity reduce drop-off.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA minimum. Keyboard navigable forms (critical for subscription flow). Reduced motion respected. High contrast for outdoor / mixed-light office use common in Philippine workplaces.
